FAQ
Common questions
What is ConstructBid AI?▼
ConstructBid AI is an AI platform that helps government contractors find, score, and win federal contracts. It auto-scans SAM.gov every 6 hours, scores opportunities against your NAICS codes, certifications, and regions, and generates proposal drafts from your company data.
Who is this for?▼
Any government contractor bidding on federal work — especially SDVOSB, 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB, and small business contractors in construction, facilities maintenance, cemetery operations, and related industries.
Do I need a SAM.gov API key?▼
Yes, but it's free. Create a SAM.gov account, go to your Profile, and request a Public API Key. Takes about 2 minutes. We have step-by-step instructions inside the app.
How does the free trial work?▼
Sign up with your email — no credit card needed. You get full access to every feature for 14 days. After that, subscribe for $299/month or your data is paused until you subscribe.
Can I cancel anytime?▼
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Manage your subscription directly from the dashboard or through Stripe's billing portal.
Is my data private?▼
Absolutely. Every company gets a completely isolated workspace. Your proposals, scores, strategies, and company data are never shared with or visible to other users.
